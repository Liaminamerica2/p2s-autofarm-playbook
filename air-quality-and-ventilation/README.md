# Air quality & ventilation

Keeping print fumes and particulates from ending up in the room while the farm runs unattended.

## Optional: ventilation and placement

- The P2S recirculates air through its built-in HEPA and carbon filter and does **not** vent outside by default.
- Bambu sells an **External Exhaust Fan Kit** (about $20). It needs printer firmware 01.01.01.00 or newer, Bambu Studio 2.4.1.80 or newer, and Bambu Handy 3.11.0 or newer. Ducting and a window adapter are extra, roughly $30 to $50 (4" duct, window panel or foam-board insert, louvered outdoor cap).
- Newer P2S units ship with an updated rear panel that has proper exhaust cutouts. Owners of the original panel could request one.
- **Avoid open-air spaces** like a patio or cabana: temperature swings, dew, dust in the AMS and fans, and filament absorbing moisture.
- Stick to PLA and PETG if the printer lives in a bedroom. Vent ABS and ASA outside.
